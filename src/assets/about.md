I am a 4th year PhD student in the [Software and Societal Systems Department](https://s3d.cmu.edu/) at Carnegie Mellon University. I am advised by [Joshua Sunshine](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jssunshi/) and [Dominik Moritz](https://www.domoritz.de/) and a member of the [Data Interaction Group](https://dig.cmu.edu/) and [Penrose](https://penrose.cs.cmu.edu/) groups. I am an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program Recipient and an ARCS Scholar.

My research interests are in the design and evaluation of intelligent systems for high-complexity tasks. Currently, I am developing systems for geometric proof that help users manage information overload through dynamic visualization, attention guidance, AI feedback generation, and formal proof checking.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a fullstack software engineer for Tableau and Tesla Motors, and received my BS in Engineering with concentrations in computing and design from Olin College of Engineering.

Outside of research, I enjoy ballet, making art, and playing video games.
